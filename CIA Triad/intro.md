<h1>What is Computer Security </h1>

The Protection afforded to an automated info system in order to attain the applicable objectives of preserving the integrity,avaliability,and confidentiality of info sys resources By NIST

<h1> CIA TRIAD </h1>

<hr>
🔐 1. Confidentiality

Definition: Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized people or systems.

Example: Passwords, encryption, and access controls prevent unauthorized users from viewing private data.

Goal: Prevent unauthorized disclosure of information.

🛡️ 2. Integrity

Definition: Ensures that information remains accurate, complete, consistent, and unaltered unless an authorized change is made.

Example: A hacker should not be able to modify a student's marks in a database without authorization.

Goal: Prevent unauthorized modification or destruction of information.

⚡ 3. Availability

Definition: Ensures that information and systems are accessible to authorized users whenever they are needed.

Example: A banking website should remain accessible to customers even during heavy traffic or hardware failures.

Goal: Prevent disruptions and ensure reliable access.

<h1>Levels Of Impact Of Security Breach </h1>

<ul type="circle">
<li><b>Low:</b> Causes minor disruption or limited damage; normal operations can continue.</li>
<li><b>Medium</b>: Causes significant disruption or data/system damage and requires corrective action.</li>
<li><b>High</b>: Causes severe or critical damage, potentially leading to major data loss, service disruption, or serious security consequences.</li>
